What is TMI™?

The Talent Magnet Index™

A diagnostic that measures the gap between how your organisation sees its own talent appeal and how the external talent market actually experiences it.

Step 1 – You
Inside-Out Assessment

20 structured questions across five dimensions. You score what actually exists in your organisation today. This becomes your internal baseline.

Step 2 – GTX
Outside-In Survey

GTX independently surveys 80+ passive professionals in your target talent segments. They score how they actually experience your organisation.

The Insight
Gap Analysis

GTX compares both perspectives across five named dimensions. The distance between them reveals where your organisation is losing ground with the talent market.

Five Dimensions

Two perspectives on the same five dimensions.

Each dimension is scored by you (Inside-Out) and by the market (Outside-In). The gap between them is where the work is.

D1
Leadership Visibility
Gap type if diverging: Transmission Gap
Inside-Out ↔ Market Recognition
⌄
Inside-Out – how you see it

How systematically your senior leaders are visible, publishing and participating in the professional communities where your critical talent operates.

Outside-In – how the market sees it

Whether passive professionals in your target segments actually recognise your leaders, follow their thinking or would name them if asked.

Transmission Gap: your investment in leadership visibility is not reaching the market.
D2
Community Depth
Gap type if diverging: Pull Gap
Inside-Out ↔ Perceived Magnetism
⌄
Inside-Out

The strength, engagement rate and relevance of the talent community your organisation has built outside formal hiring.

Outside-In

Whether passive professionals feel a gravitational pull toward your organisation as a professional destination – independent of any open role.

Pull Gap: the community exists but is not creating sufficient gravitational interest externally.
D3
Pipeline Health
Gap type if diverging: Signal Gap
Inside-Out ↔ Brand Accuracy
⌄
Inside-Out

How ready your existing talent pipeline is for critical roles before a vacancy opens – warm relationships or starting from zero every time.

Outside-In

Whether the market holds an accurate picture of you as an employer, or passive professionals carry misconceptions that prevent them from considering you.

Signal Gap: your brand signals are not landing accurately in the market.
D4
Employer Brand Trust
Gap type if diverging: Conversion Gap
Inside-Out ↔ Candidate Readiness
⌄
Inside-Out

How positively your organisation is perceived by professionals who don't yet work for you, and whether that perception is actively managed and measured.

Outside-In

Whether passive professionals would genuinely consider a conversation with your organisation if the right opportunity arose.

Conversion Gap: brand trust is not translating into candidate willingness to engage.
D5
Conversion Efficiency
Gap type if diverging: Experience Gap
Inside-Out ↔ Process Reputation
⌄
Inside-Out

How efficiently relationship investment converts into accepted offers and retained hires – pipeline-sourced vs market-sourced performance over time.

Outside-In

The market's perception of your candidate experience and whether professionals actively recommend or warn against engaging with your organisation.

Experience Gap: candidate experience is undermining relationship investment at the point of conversion.
