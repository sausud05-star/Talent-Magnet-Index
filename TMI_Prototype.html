How this works
Two surveys. One gap analysis. One report you can actually use.
PART 1 · 10-12 MIN
Insider-Out
20 questions about what your org has systematically built. Scored 1 (not in place) to 4 (exemplary and measured).
PART 2 · 8-10 MIN
Outsider-In
Same 5 dimensions, answered from the perspective of a talented professional in your target market. Scored 1-5.
The key scoring rule
Score what is systematically in place, not what was done once. Default to lower scores when uncertain. Honest answers produce the most useful gap analysis.
You will see your Part 1 score immediately after question 20. This is the hook that earns Part 2. The gap between the two scores is the strategic insight this report produces.

Top 3 investment priorities
1. Employer brand trust
Your Glassdoor presence and careers content are not yet building passive trust. A systematic review response process, monthly employee stories, and a referral programme above 40% would move this dimension meaningfully within 6 months.
2. Community depth
Professionals in your target segment don't yet experience your organisation outside of a job listing. A quarterly community touchpoint — even a focused roundtable or webinar — begins building the ambient presence that converts to pipeline over 12-18 months.
3. Conversion efficiency
Tracking offer decline reasons systematically is the first step. Pipeline candidates should convert at 2x+ the cold-sourced rate. If they're not, the investment in building a pipeline is not yet returning.
