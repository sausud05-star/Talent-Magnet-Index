Why this matters specifically for senior and specialist talent ⌄

Senior professionals move through trusted networks, not job boards. When a critical vacancy opens at Director level or above, the most qualified candidates have almost never applied to you. The question GTX helps you answer is not "how do we find better candidates?" It is "does the talent market know, trust and would consider us before we need them?"

That is a fundamentally different — and more strategically valuable — question than conventional talent acquisition.

Explore each component in detail ⌄
01 TMI™ / Insider-Out
A structured 20-question self-assessment completed by one senior leader (CHRO, CEO or CPO). Scores your organisation across five dimensions on a 1–4 scale. Produces a composite 0–100 Talent Magnet score and your internal baseline. Takes 15–20 minutes.
02 Outsider-In
GTX administers a parallel survey to a curated panel of 80+ passive professionals in your target talent segments. Zero prior relationship required. Produces the external perspective on the same five dimensions.
03 Gap Analysis
GTX compares both surveys and identifies five specific gap types: Transmission Gap (D1), Pull Gap (D2), Signal Gap (D3), Conversion Gap (D4) and Experience Gap (D5). Each gap tells a specific story about where internal investment is not transmitting to the market.
04 Gap to Magnet™
GTX designs a talent community and engagement programme around your specific gaps — not a generic platform. Passive professionals in your target talent segments build genuine familiarity with your organisation before a role exists. The community becomes a warm talent network over time.
05 Annual Remeasure
GTX re-runs both surveys annually. Progress is tracked across the same five dimensions. The system compounds: as perception improves, engagement increases, the pipeline warms, and hiring becomes progressively more relationship-led.
Five Dimensions

Two perspectives on the same five dimensions.

Each dimension is scored internally (Insider-Out) and externally (Outsider-In). The gap between them is where the work is.

D1
Leadership Visibility
Insider-Out  ↔  Market Recognition
Gap type: Transmission Gap
⌄
Insider-Out — how you see it

How systematically your senior leaders are visible, publishing and participating in the professional communities where your critical talent operates.

Outsider-In — how the market sees it

Whether passive professionals in your target segments actually recognise your leaders, follow their thinking or would name them if asked about your organisation.

Gap type when diverging: Transmission Gap — your investment in leadership visibility is not reaching the market.
D2
Community Depth
Insider-Out  ↔  Perceived Magnetism
⌄
Insider-Out

The strength, engagement rate and professional relevance of the talent community your organisation has built outside formal hiring.

Outsider-In

Whether passive professionals experience a gravitational pull toward your organisation as a professional destination — independent of any open role.

Gap type when diverging: Pull Gap — the community exists but is not creating sufficient gravitational interest externally.
D3
Pipeline Health
Insider-Out  ↔  Brand Accuracy
⌄
Insider-Out

How ready your existing talent pipeline is for critical roles before a vacancy opens — whether you have warm, pre-qualified relationships or start every search from zero.

Outsider-In

Whether the market's picture of you as an employer is consistent and accurate, or whether passive professionals hold misconceptions that prevent them from considering you.

Gap type when diverging: Signal Gap — your brand signals are not landing accurately in the market.
D4
Employer Brand Trust
Insider-Out  ↔  Candidate Readiness
⌄
Insider-Out

How positively your organisation is perceived by professionals who don't yet work for you, and whether that perception is actively managed and measured.

Outsider-In

Whether passive professionals would genuinely consider approaching your organisation if the right opportunity arose — their authentic openness to a conversation.

Gap type when diverging: Conversion Gap — brand trust is not translating into candidate willingness to engage.
D5
Conversion Efficiency
Insider-Out  ↔  Process Reputation
⌄
Insider-Out

How efficiently relationship investment converts into accepted offers and retained hires — whether pipeline-sourced candidates outperform market-sourced candidates over time.

Outsider-In

The market's perception of your candidate experience, hiring process fairness and whether professionals actively recommend or warn against engaging with your organisation.

Gap type when diverging: Experience Gap — candidate experience is undermining relationship investment at the point of conversion.
